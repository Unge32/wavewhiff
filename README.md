**wavewhiff** (noun, WAYV-whif) — *waving back at a stranger who was actually waving at someone behind you.*

One invented English word, defined in a single clear line of 84 characters. Everyone has done it: the wave goes up, the smile lands, and then you see the stranger's eyes slide past you to the person behind you. English had no word for that moment. Now it does: wavewhiff. From *wave* + *whiff* (baseball slang: to swing and miss) — you whiffed the wave.

Example: *I wavewhiffed at the cashier this morning — turns out she was waving at her coworker.*

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the word + definition line to `out/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
